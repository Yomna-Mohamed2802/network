6.1 Reading: How Robots Think, Move and Work

  • Robotics is a branch of AI and engineering that designs and builds machines that do tasks automatically.الروبوتكس فرع من الذكاء الاصطناعي والهندسة بيصمم ويبني آلات بتشتغل لوحدها.
  • A robot senses, thinks and acts: it uses sensors to understand its environment, algorithms to decide, and motors or wheels to move.الروبوت بيحس (sensors)، ويفكر (algorithms)، ويتحرك (موتورات وعجل).
  • Automation = using technology to do repetitive tasks without human help. Automated systems follow programmed instructions and may not "think"; intelligent robots can adapt to changes.الـ automation: تكنولوجيا بتعمل مهام متكررة من غير بني آدم، بتنفّذ أوامر بس. الروبوت الذكي بيتأقلم مع التغيير.
  • Types: industrial robots assemble products in factories; service robots clean houses or help customers; medical robots do surgeries or deliver medicine; exploration robots study dangerous or distant places (space, ocean).أنواعها: صناعية (تجميع في المصانع)، وخدمية (تنضيف ومساعدة عملاء)، وطبية (عمليات وتوصيل أدوية)، واستكشاف (الفضاء والمحيط).
  • Robotics improves speed, accuracy and safety but raises questions about jobs and ethics; the future of work depends on how humans and robots cooperate.بتزوّد السرعة والدقة والأمان، بس بتفتح أسئلة عن الوظايف والأخلاق.
Comprehension questions with model answers
  1. What is robotics? A branch of AI and engineering that designs and builds machines that perform tasks automatically.
  2. How does a robot sense and act? It uses sensors to understand its environment, makes decisions with algorithms, and moves with mechanical parts such as motors or wheels.
  3. Robotics vs automation? Automation uses technology to repeat programmed tasks without human help and may not "think"; intelligent robots can adapt to changes in their surroundings.
  4. Four types: industrial (assemble products), service (clean houses, assist customers), medical (surgery, deliver medicine), exploration (study space or the ocean).
  5. Advantages and challenges: more speed, accuracy and safety; but questions about jobs and ethics.

6.2 Grammar: The Passive Voice (Present and Past)

  • Use the passive when the action or the object is more important than who does it (processes, inventions).بنستخدم المبني للمجهول لما الفعل نفسه أهم من مين عمله.
  • Present simple passive: am / is / are + past participle (V3). "Robots are programmed to follow instructions."مضارع: am/is/are + التصريف التالت.
  • Past simple passive: was / were + V3. "The first industrial robot was created in 1959."ماضي: was/were + التصريف التالت.
  • The doer can be added with by: "The robot's movements are controlled by an onboard computer."الفاعل لو عايزين نذكره بنحطه بعد by.
Active:  Engineers program the robots.        →  Passive: The robots are programmed (by engineers).
Active:  The company built a new model.       →  Passive: A new model was built (by the company).
object of the active → subject of the passive;  verb → be (same tense) + V3
Book practices 1–3 (answers)
P1: 1 are used   2 was developed   3 are installed   4 are controlled   5 are assembled
P2: 1 The robots are programmed by engineers.
    2 A new model was built by the company last year.
    3 Motion is detected by the sensors.
    4 The robot was designed by scientists for medical use.
    5 The factory is cleaned manually by workers.
P3: 1 is built   2 are used   3 was designed   4 is controlled   5 are done
في P1 الجملة 2 فيها "early 2000s" يعني ماضي → was developed. وفي P3 الفاعل الحقيقي مش موجود (الآلة مش هي اللي بتبني)، فالمبني للمجهول هو الصح.
غلطات بتتسأل: ‎"was design" غلط → was designed (لازم V3). ‎"are using" معناها هما اللي بيستخدموا، والصح are used. ولازم الـ be تتفق مع الفاعل: Robots are… / The robot is…

6.6 Writing: How Robots and Automation Are Changing Human Work

Plan: Introduction (definitions) → Examples → Positive effects → Negative effects → Conclusion. Use the passive and modals.الخطة: تعريفات، أمثلة، مميزات، عيوب، خاتمة. واستخدمي الـ passive والـ modals.

Model essay (about 170 words)

Robotics is a branch of artificial intelligence that designs machines which can sense, think and act. Automation means using technology to do repetitive tasks without human help. Both are changing the way people work.

Today, robots are used in many places. For example, cars are assembled by industrial robots, houses are cleaned by service robots, and some operations are performed by medical robots. As a result, work is done faster and with more precision, and dangerous jobs are given to machines instead of people.

However, automation also creates problems. Some workers lose their jobs, so they must learn new skills. Companies should also think about safety and ethics, because robots can make mistakes if their sensors fail.

In conclusion, robots will not replace humans completely. Humans and robots have to collaborate: robots can do repetitive tasks, while people control, maintain and improve them.

لاحظي: are used، are assembled، is done (passive) و must، should، have to (modals) وروابط For example، As a result، However، In conclusion.

7.1 Reading: The Moral Side of Artificial Intelligence

  • As AI becomes more powerful it raises ethical questions: should a machine make life-changing decisions? How can AI be used fairly and safely?كل ما الـ AI بيقوى، بتطلع أسئلة أخلاقية: ينفع آلة تاخد قرارات مصيرية؟
  • AI ethics = the study of right and wrong in the design and use of intelligent systems. Key values: fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy.أخلاقيات الـ AI = الصح والغلط في تصميم واستخدام الأنظمة الذكية. القيم: العدل، والشفافية، والمسؤولية، والخصوصية.
  • Examples of problems: a hiring algorithm may reject applicants unfairly because it learned from biased data; facial recognition may violate privacy if used without consent.أمثلة: برنامج توظيف بيرفض ناس ظلم عشان اتعلم من داتا متحيزة، والتعرف على الوشوش ممكن ينتهك الخصوصية من غير موافقة.
  • Ethical AI needs human oversight, clear rules and responsible design; developers must make sure systems respect human rights and social values.الـ AI الأخلاقي محتاج إشراف بشري وقواعد وتصميم مسؤول.
  • Governments and organizations create AI ethics guidelines to prevent misuse and build public trust. The goal is not to stop AI but to make it responsible, inclusive and beneficial.الهدف مش إننا نوقف الـ AI، الهدف نخليه مسؤول ومفيد للكل.

  2. Why is fairness important? Because AI that learns from biased data can make unfair decisions, for example rejecting job applicants unfairly.
  3. Examples of unethical AI behavior: a biased hiring algorithm; facial recognition used without consent.
  4. How can human oversight help? People can monitor AI, find mistakes and prevent misuse.
  5. Main goal of ethical AI: to make AI responsible, inclusive and beneficial for everyone, not to stop it.

7.2 Grammar: The First Conditional

  • Form: If + present simple, will + base verb. It talks about real, possible future situations and their consequences.الشكل: If + مضارع بسيط، will + فعل في المصدر. للحاجات اللي ممكن تحصل فعلاً في المستقبل.
  • "If AI becomes too powerful, it will need strict control." The clauses can swap: "AI will need strict control if it becomes too powerful."ممكن نبدّل الجزئين، ولو If في الأول بنحط فاصلة.
  • Never put will in the if-clause: ✗ If AI will be trained… ✓ If AI is trained…ممنوع will بعد If.
  • unless = if … not: "Unless governments regulate AI, misuse will continue."unless = لو مش.
Book practices 1–3 (answers)
P1: 1 will help   2 will need   3 will be   4 will be   5 will trust
P2: 1 If data is biased, AI will make mistakes.
    2 If we test systems, they will become safer.
    3 If developers hide problems, users will lose trust.
P3: 1 is … will make   2 don't protect … will (misuse)   3 will trust … is   4 create … will
في P3 الجملة 4: create مع will (First). created مع would ده الـ Second Conditional ومش موضوعنا.

  • Use: responsibility ("AI systems must be tested before release") and recommendations ("Data should be protected by developers").للمسؤولية والنصايح.

Book practices 4–5 (answers)
P4: 1 must be tested   2 should be protected   3 can be designed   4 might be caused   5 must be respected

    2 User information should be protected by companies.

7.6 Writing: Why Ethical AI Matters for the Future

Opinion essay plan: Introduction (clear opinion) → 2–3 reasons → one example → conclusion. Use the first conditional and the passive with modals.مقال رأي: رأيك بوضوح، وأسباب، ومثال، وخاتمة.

Model essay (about 170 words)

In my opinion, ethical AI is one of the most important goals for the future, because AI is now used to make decisions that affect people's lives.

First, AI must be fair. If a system learns from biased data, it will make unfair decisions. For example, a hiring algorithm might reject good applicants only because of their gender or background.

Second, privacy should be protected. Facial recognition can be used to follow people without their consent. If companies collect data without permission, people will lose trust in technology.

Third, someone must be accountable. When a self-driving car causes an accident, users need to know who is responsible. That's why human oversight and clear regulations are needed.

In conclusion, we should not stop AI, but it must be designed and used responsibly. If developers follow ethical rules, AI will be safer, fairer and more useful for everyone.

لاحظي: If … will (First conditional) و must be designed، should be protected، can be used (passive with modals).

8.1 Reading: The Power of Data in Artificial Intelligence

  • Every intelligent system depends on data. Data science = collecting, organizing and analyzing information to make useful decisions. With AI, it lets machines learn from examples instead of only following commands.علم البيانات: نجمع ونرتب ونحلل المعلومات عشان ناخد قرارات. ومع الـ AI الآلة بتتعلم من الأمثلة.

  • for + a period (for two weeks, for a year); since + a starting point (since January, since last month).for + مدة. since + بداية.

    4 has been using   5 has been developing

8.6 Writing: How Data Shapes Artificial Intelligence

Informative text: Introduction (definition) → Explanation (the four steps) → Two examples → Benefits and challenges → Conclusion.مقال معلوماتي: تعريف، والخطوات، ومثالين، والمميزات والتحديات، وخاتمة.

Model essay (about 170 words)

Data science is the process of collecting, organizing and analyzing information to make useful decisions. Today, it is the fuel of artificial intelligence, because AI learns from examples, not only from commands.

The process has four steps. First, a lot of raw data is collected from different sources. Then the data is cleaned, so mistakes and irrelevant items are removed. After that, it is analyzed to find patterns. Finally, models use these patterns to make predictions.

There are many examples in daily life. A weather app has been analyzing years of climate data to predict tomorrow's temperature, and streaming services recommend movies based on our viewing history.

Data brings many benefits: faster decisions and higher accuracy. However, there are a few challenges. If the data is biased or dirty, the results will be wrong, and personal data can be misused.

In conclusion, data shapes how AI thinks, so it must be collected and used responsibly.

لاحظي الروابط First، Then، After that، Finally والـ quantifiers ‏a lot of، a few، many والـ present perfect continuous ‏has been analyzing.
